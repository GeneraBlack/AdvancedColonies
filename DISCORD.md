# 🏰 Advanced Colonies — Now Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)!

**Advanced Colonies** is an all-in-one AI, pathfinding, worker, and immersion expansion for **MineColonies**, engineered from the ground up with clean Mixin hooks (zero `@Overwrite` conflicts) and strict server-enforced limits.

### ✨ Highlights
* 🧭 **Next-Gen Pathfinding & Self-Healing Stuck Recovery** — Manhattan tie-breaker heuristics, road speed boosts, safe vertical traversal (rails, ladders, vines, scaffolding), and a 3-tier recovery system that rescues stuck citizens without lethal fall-damage teleports.
* 🗣️ **Context-Reactive Voices & 3D Speech Bubbles** — **376 emotional voice clips** across 8 unique citizen voice profiles (`male1–4`, `female1–4`) + **380+ dynamic English voicelines** that react to raids, weather, hunger, housing, sickness, and all 28 professions.
* 🔨 **Smart Builders & Crafters** — Dynamic builder build/break reach (`+1` to `+20` blocks), fluid source cleanup (prevents water/lava griefing), and automatic inventory unblocking for crafters & builders.
* ⚔️ **Tactical Guard Patrols & Raid Scaling** — Multi-guard patrol dispersion (no more single-file stacking), safe combat leaping, configurable guard tower bonus range, and fair difficulty scaling.
* 🔍 **Live 3D In-Game AI Debugger** — Visualize live A* paths, stuck recovery beacons, and citizen job states in 3D (`F8` toggle or `/acdebug`).

📦 **Requirements:** Minecraft `1.21.1` | NeoForge `21.1.200+` | MineColonies `1.1.1285+`
